
===== Типичный пример: каталог /tmp =====

$ ls -ld /tmp
drwxrwxrwt 2 root root 4096 Oct 25 22:31 /tmp

===== Каталог без sticky-бита: чужой файл удаляется =====

$ mkdir -p /tmp/experiments
$ echo "Hello" > /tmp/experiments/file.txt
$ sudo chown alice /tmp/experiments/file.txt
$ ls -la /tmp/experiments
total 12
drwxr-xr-x 2 bob   bob  4096 Oct 25 22:31 .
drwxrwxrwt 3 root  root 4096 Oct 25 22:31 ..
-rw-r--r-- 1 alice bob     6 Oct 25 22:31 file.txt
$ whoami
bob
$ rm /tmp/experiments/file.txt
$ ls -l /tmp/experiments/file.txt
ls: cannot access '/tmp/experiments/file.txt': No such file or directory

===== Каталог со sticky-битом: чужой файл удалить нельзя =====

$ echo "Hello" > /tmp/file2.txt
$ sudo chown alice /tmp/file2.txt
$ ls -l /tmp/file2.txt
-rw-r--r-- 1 alice bob 6 Oct 25 22:31 /tmp/file2.txt
$ rm /tmp/file2.txt
rm: cannot remove '/tmp/file2.txt': Operation not permitted
